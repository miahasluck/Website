hello i’m mia hasluck, welcome to my office. i’m a visual artist based in london. my practise begins through digital illustration & design, but manifests physically through print & dissemination. but not all of my projects fit in these categories. i love bootlegging, reading, satirical work & designing in rgb. i dislike academic jargon, messy desktops & the pub tattooed on my arm. i try where i can to inject joyful activism & colourful intervention into contemporary culture. if you’d like to work together, please contact me!
